## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, deliberate pacing, and rich physicality of an artisan press fused with a decentralized creator ecosystem. It rejects cold, clinical tech minimalism in favor of a tactile, bookish sanctuary: heavy cream paper stock, aged golden honey hues, and deep timber tones.

The aesthetic merges **Tactile Editorial** with **Soft Modernism**. Interfaces read like a luxury bound periodical: deliberate proportions, deep optical margins, subtle ink-like typography, and gilded accents that reward patronage and deep focus.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from apiaries, natural flax fiber, and autumnal presses.

- **Paper Layers:** The foundational viewport uses Base Paper (`#FAF5EC`). Structural framing, side rails, and recessed groupings use Raised Panel (`#F3ECDD`). Interactive reading surfaces and elevated modules employ Card Surface (`#FFFDF6`), bounded by Warm Line Borders (`#E7DCC4`).
- **Typography & Ink:** Text mimics woodblock ink. Primary reading copy and high-emphasis labels rely on Deep Wood Brown (`#3B2B1F`). Subtitles, metadata, and reading time are rendered in Muted Bark (`#8A7A66`). Dark Wood (`#2E2018`) provides grounding weight in the masthead hero and full-width footer.
- **Honey & Harvest Accents:** Primary interactions rely on Honey (`#E8A33D`) transitioning to Deep Honey (`#C97F1F`) on hover and active states. Autumn Amber (`#B8742A`) and subtle honey-amber gradients (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E8A33D 0%, #B8742A 100%)`) signify patron-supported and premium publications.
- **Semantics & Badges:** Foliage Green (`#6B8E4E`) signals public access, verified creator states, and organic distribution. Rust Harvest (`#A6552A`) handles critical alerts, errors, and unconfirmed states without disrupting the organic visual ecosystem.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy bridges literary heritage and decentralized technology.

- **Display & Headlines (Newsreader):** Set in high-character editorial serif with medium-to-bold weights (600–700) and optical sizing. Tight negative tracking gives titles a sculpted, physical press appearance. Editorial italics are reserved for epigraphs, pull quotes, and author sub-titles.
- **Body Text (Source Sans 3):** Clean humanist rhythm optimized for sustained, fatigue-free longform reading on backlit displays. Body copy features an expanded line height (1.65–1.7x) to honor book design conventions.
- **Protocol Identifiers (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced characters ground decentralized primitives (Swarm content hashes, Arkiv immutability stamps, ENS handles, Avalanche transaction hashes) with technical credibility without conflicting with editorial prose.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a calibrated 12-column grid with generous margins reminiscent of traditional book margins.

- **Reading Canvas:** Longform article streams enforce a strict maximum reading column of `680px` to maintain optimal typographic line length (60–75 characters per line).
- **Feed & Discovery:** Employs an asymmetric 12-column grid. Curated and pinned stories span 8 columns, while archival metadata, provenance tokens, and author notes span 4 columns.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** Full 12-column grid, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Dual-rail support for table of contents and creator actions.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Sidebars collapse into dismissible drawer sheets.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack, 20px margins, 16px gutters. Fixed top bar transitions into reading progress indicator.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic digital dropshadows, instead using **tonal stacking** and **warm, low-opacity ambient shadows** that mimic diffuse natural daylight through beeswax paper.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#FAF5EC` background.
- **Level 1 (Structural Insets & Trays):** `#F3ECDD` with a 1px solid `#E7DCC4` border. No drop shadow.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Active Panels):** `#FFFDF6` elevated with `0 2px 8px rgba(59, 43, 31, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(59, 43, 31, 0.02)` and a crisp 1px `#E7DCC4` border.
- **Level 3 (Floating Overlays & Menus):** `#FFFDF6` background with `0 12px 32px rgba(59, 43, 31, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(59, 43, 31, 0.04)` enclosed in a 1px `#E7DCC4` contour.
- **Gated Overlays:** Gated content blocks use multi-stop honey veil gradients (`rgba(250, 245, 236, 0.3)` to `rgba(243, 236, 221, 0.95)`) backed by a `12px` Gaussian blur, locking unreleased reading segments under a warm amber glaze.

## Shapes

The interface honors bookbinding and paper stationery, utilizing crisp, understated radii.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to form fields, technical code chips, and small metadata modules. Evokes deckled-edge paper precision.
- **Containers & Cards (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Medium rounding applied to reading cards, modal dialogs, and content panels.
- **Full Radius (Pill):** Strictly reserved for protocol badges, subscription toggles, tag chips, and the primary Connect Wallet button.

## Components

### Navigation Bar
Sticky `#FAF5EC` surface with an anchor border of 1px `#E7DCC4`. Houses the masthead wordmark with a honey-colored period (`Nectar<span style="color: #E8A33D">.</span>`) in bold Newsreader. Right-aligned items feature category links, reading list counters, and the wallet connection pill.

### Connect Wallet Button
A pill-shaped button wrapped in an active 1px border (`#E7DCC4`). Default state: `#FFFDF6` background, Deep Wood Brown text, displaying the active ENS handle in `JetBrains Mono` accompanied by a green status pip (`#6B8E4E`). Hover state transitions to Deep Wood Dark `#2E2018` with cream text.

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Button:** Solid honey gold (`#E8A33D`), text `#3B2B1F` in 600 weight. Active/hover transitions smoothly to deep honey (`#C97F1F`).
- **Secondary / Gilded Button:** Soft amber gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FAF5EC 0%, #F3ECDD 100%)`), outlined by Autumn Amber (`#B8742A`), text in Autumn Amber.
- **Ghost Button:** Zero background fill, Deep Wood Brown text, soft `#F3ECDD` background on hover.

### Article Card
Constructed on Card Surface (`#FFFDF6`) with a 1px `#E7DCC4` perimeter and a subtle hover translation (`translateY(-2px)`). 
- **Header:** Byline displaying author avatar, ENS/Arkiv address badge in JetBrains Mono, and Foliage Green (`#6B8E4E`) open-access pill.
- **Body:** Newsreader headline in `#3B2B1F`, followed by a 2-line excerpt in `#8A7A66`.
- **Footer:** Reading time, Swarm publication timestamp, and category tag pills with a `#FAF5EC` fill and `#E7DCC4` border.

### Gated Teaser Card
Truncated article container with an ambient gradient overlay dissolving into `#F3ECDD`. A centered amber-gilded card (`#FFFDF6` with 1px `#B8742A` border) presents access requirements (patron subscription or collectible token), featuring an Autumn Amber lock icon, unlock pricing, and an amber gradient action button.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
Inputs sit on `#FAF5EC` recessed background, shifting to `#FFFDF6` on focus with a 1.5px `#E8A33D` outline and no harsh glow. Checkboxes and radio controls feature warm wood brown borders with solid honey fills and cream check marks when active.

### Global Footer
Immersive Dark Wood (`#2E2018`) spanning full width. Typography shifts to Warm Cream (`#FAF5EC`) and Muted Bark (`#8A7A66`). Contains decentralized protocol acknowledgments (Swarm decentralized storage, Arkiv entity graphs, Avalanche settlement, and ENSv2 resolution) labeled in subtle `JetBrains Mono` badges alongside editorial links.
